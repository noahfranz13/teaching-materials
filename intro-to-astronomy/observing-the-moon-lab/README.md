# Observing the Moon Lab

This lab is intended for an introduction to astronomy course (math 
based but not calculus, for first or second year students). The general 
goal is to (1) have students look at the sky and (2) try to have students
begin to understand how the position of the moon cooresponds to an orbit.
To do this, students will observe the moon at dusk each night and, if
the moon is visible, record the approximate altitude and azimuth, and
estimate the uncertainty on each.
